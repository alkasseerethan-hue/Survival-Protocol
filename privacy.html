Privacy Policy

Survival Protocol respects your privacy.

Information We Collect

When you use YouTube verification, Google may provide information needed to verify whether your YouTube account is subscribed to our channel.

How We Use Information

We use this information only to verify YouTube subscription status and to prevent unauthorized voting.

Google and YouTube

YouTube verification uses Google's OAuth authentication system. We do not ask for or store your Google password.

Data Storage

Voting information and verification session information may be stored temporarily to operate the Survival Protocol voting system.

Contact

For privacy questions, please contact the developer through the contact information provided with the application.
